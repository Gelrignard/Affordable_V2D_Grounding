BALANCE IS STILL CONTROLLED

Zero leg residual ≠ frozen legs

SONIC sees current body feedback and keeps producing leg targets. Our pilot leaves these targets to SONIC while learning manipulation corrections.

It is a motion-tracking prior, not a guarantee against falling. Manipulation loads and poor references can still destabilize it. The simulator keeps full-body dynamics and fall termination.

Our model learns the response of SONIC + residual wrapper + physics together, from actual transitions. No dynamics labels are invented from the corrected reference.
